LEVER 01 • SEMANTIC SPEC PRECISION

Numeric Spec Matching

Rufus prioritizes explicit technical specifications (wattage, speed limits, incline % in bullet points) over marketing hyperbole when answering user comparison prompts.

LEVER 02 • DEFENSIVE SAFEGUARDS

Substitution Resistance

When shoppers ask "Are there better alternatives?", Rufus analyzes customer review sentiment clusters to confirm or counter the switch recommendation.

LEVER 03 • OFFENSIVE CONQUESTING

Competitor Page Interception

Positioning compact footprint and quiet decibel ratings on PDP imagery allows Rufus to intercept competitor traffic directly when asked for small-apartment treadmills.
