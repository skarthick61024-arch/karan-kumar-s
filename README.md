## 📂 Core Disciplines & Modules

### 01. Product Listing Architecture (Amazon PDP & 3x3 Creative Matrix)
- Interactive 3x3 visual hierarchy simulator evaluating Hero packaging, infographic callouts, comparison charts, and trust badges.
- Dedicated modal with live product filtering (PT500, SX175, WRX500, SP800) and multi-angle inspection.

### 02. A+ Brand Story & E-Commerce Modules
- Interactive Before/After drag comparison slider showcasing raw renders vs. commercial production layouts.
- Premium modular story cards with 3D tilt, glare effects, and German/English dual-language positioning for European sports-tech brands.

### 03. Shopify Merchandising & E-Comm Product Grid
- High-converting direct-to-consumer store layouts, value proposition callouts, and conversion-optimized visual hierarchy.

### 04. AI Generation Pipelines & Production Workflows
- Enterprise generative workflows utilizing Midjourney v6, ComfyUI node graphs, Kling LoRA face consistency, and prompt deconstructions.
- Side-by-side prompt breakdowns with generation metadata and fidelity scores.

### 05. Direct-Response Video & UGC Reels
- High-performing commercial Reels and TikTok creative formats with metric overlays (ROAS, thumb-stop rate, CTR, average watch time).
- Embedded interactive Video Theater modal with custom player controls.

### 06. Technical Case Studies Vault
- **Red Bull F1 Spec Film**: Cinematic multi-shot AI production pipeline with camera motion choreography.
- **Amazon Rufus Reverse-Engineering**: Visual semantic indexing and multimodal conversational AI optimization.
- **Higgsfield AI Node Pipeline**: Node graph architectures for scalable brand content creation.
- **Model Benchmarks & Synthesis**: Quantitative comparisons across Midjourney v6, Kling, Flux, and SDXL.

### 07. Performance Social Media & Meta Funnel Architecture
- Full-funnel Meta paid ads strategy (Top of Funnel Hook &rarr; Mid Funnel Education &rarr; Bottom of Funnel Urgency).
- 4K commercial and event poster gallery with interactive Lightbox zoom.
